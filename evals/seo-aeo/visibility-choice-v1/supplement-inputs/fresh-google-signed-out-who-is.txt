Kaleb Cole is an American white supremacist and the former leader of the Washington state chapter of the neo-Nazi group Atomwaffen Division.
Department of Justice (.gov) (+1)

In January 2022, Cole was sentenced to seven years (84 months) in federal prison following his conviction on five felony charges, including conspiracy, mailing threatening communications, and interfering with a federally protected activity. The convictions stemmed from a multi-state plot in 2020 known as "Operation Erste Säule," where Cole and his co-conspirators targeted Jewish individuals, community advocates, and journalists of color. The group mailed or glued swastika-laden posters to the victims' homes featuring threatening messages such as "You have been visited by your local Nazis" and "We are Watching".

Background and Extremist Activities
Cole joined the Atomwaffen Division in 2016 and quickly rose to a leadership role within its Washington state operations. According to law enforcement and watchdog organizations like the Anti-Defamation League, Cole was heavily involved in organizing "Hate Camps"—paramilitary training grounds where group members practiced firearms drills in preparation for what they believed was an impending "race war".

Legal History
2019 Weapon Seizure: Prior to his federal arrest, Cole was the subject of an Extreme Risk Protection Order (a "red flag" law) requested by the FBI and local authorities in Snohomish County, Washington. Law enforcement stripped him of an AK-47 rifle, multiple handguns, and high-capacity magazines after documenting him as an imminent threat to public safety.
YouTube · KING 5 Seattle (+1)
2021 Trial and 2022 Sentencing: Cole was arrested by federal authorities in February 2020. During his September 2021 trial, federal prosecutors presented evidence of the intimidation plot, including video captured by an undercover FBI agent showing Cole discussing the harassment campaign. The jury deliberated for just 90 minutes before finding him guilty on all counts. He received his seven-year prison sentence on January 11, 2022.
