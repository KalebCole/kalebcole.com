# Writing from draft to verified live

1. Follow `AGENTS.md`'s reading order and the owner-decisions list in `skills/kalebcole-site/SKILL.md`.
2. Read `references/writing.md` and follow the requested preparation, review, or edit scope.
3. When publication is requested, ship with `skills/kalebcole-site/references/shipping.md`. Continue to the post-live steps only after its production verification passes.

## Post-live steps

1. Fetch the production page and confirm `og:title`, `og:description`, and `og:image` are present and that the image URL returns 200. Report and resolve preview problems before handing off.
2. Hand the live link and key points from the owner's words off to `skills/post-blog-to-socials/SKILL.md`.
