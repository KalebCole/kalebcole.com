# Post a live blog to socials

1. Confirm the post is live: load the public URL and continue only when it returns the piece.
2. Collect the live link plus 3-5 key points quoted or closely taken from the author's own text. Do not rewrite them in the author's voice.
3. Hand the link and key points to whatever drafts X and LinkedIn posts for the author (the author's X and LinkedIn assistants, by message). Ask each to draft in its own voice.
4. Do not draft, send, or post the X or LinkedIn copy yourself. Nothing goes out without the author's ok, because posting speaks in the author's name.
5. Report what was handed off.
